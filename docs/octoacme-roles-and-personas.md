# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads / Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide engineering execution, technical decision-making, and implementation quality across one or more teams. They translate product goals into sustainable technical approaches and help teams navigate complex delivery challenges.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, architecture patterns, and design constraints
- Review technology trade-offs with engineering, PM, and Product teams
- Support design discussions, refactoring, and technical risk mitigation
- Help unblock complex dependencies and implementation issues
- Validate that solutions are maintainable, testable, and production-ready

### Goals
- Deliver robust, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical risk and rework
- Align implementation quality with product and business priorities

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design meetings
- Technical risk updates in standups and sprint planning
- Escalation paths for dependency or feasibility issues

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work with Product Managers to assess feasibility and trade-offs before commitment
- Partner with Project Managers to surface delivery risks, dependencies, and resource gaps
- Guide Developers on architecture, standards, and technical quality expectations
- Coordinate with QA Leads to ensure testability and release confidence
- Escalate to Security / Compliance partners when technical decisions affect risk or compliance

---

## Engineering Managers / Delivery Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Delivery Managers support team health, delivery capacity, and execution flow. They focus on planning, team coordination, and removal of operating friction so the project can advance predictably.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity, workload balancing, and staffing considerations
- Coordinate cross-team dependencies and handoffs
- Support sprint execution health and delivery forecasting
- Help identify process bottlenecks and improve operational efficiency
- Partner with Project Managers on planning and risk tracking

### Goals
- Keep engineering delivery predictable and sustainable
- Improve team productivity and cross-functional coordination
- Reduce unnecessary blockers and escalations

### Typical Communication
- Team planning and delivery check-ins
- Dependency reviews with project and product stakeholders
- Cross-team coordination on resourcing and scheduling

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work closely with Project Managers to align delivery plans, milestones, and risks
- Support Product Managers by translating roadmap priorities into feasible work plans
- Coordinate with Technical Leads to ensure engineering work matches capacity and technical constraints
- Partner with Developers on team health, work allocation, and execution flow
- Escalate issues to stakeholders when resourcing or dependency blockers threaten outcomes

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality standards, testing strategy, and validation practices. They ensure that quality is built into delivery and that release decisions are based on objective evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality gates, test strategy, and validation approach
- Review acceptance criteria and identify test coverage gaps
- Coordinate manual and automated testing across critical flows
- Report quality risks and release readiness to stakeholders
- Support defect triage and regression prevention

### Goals
- Deliver high-confidence releases with fewer defects in production
- Improve test coverage and validation quality across the project lifecycle
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or incomplete quality criteria

### Typical Communication
- Review sessions with Product and Engineering teams
- Defect and release-readiness reports
- Validation updates during sprint demos and release checkpoints

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria are testable and complete
- Partner with Developers on defect triage, regression prevention, and validation design
- Support Project Managers by assessing delivery risk and release readiness
- Coordinate with Technical Leads on testability of architecture and critical integrations
- Share release confidence signals with stakeholders and Release Managers

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance partners identify and manage risk associated with data handling, secure design, operational controls, and policy requirements. They protect the organization and customers while helping teams move safely and responsibly.

### Responsibilities
- Review project risks related to data protection, security, and compliance
- Provide guidance on secure design, threat modeling, and control implementation
- Validate that work meets policy, legal, and organizational standards
- Support escalation for urgent or high-risk security concerns
- Help define mitigation plans and remediation priorities

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance exposure
- Enable teams to ship safely without avoidable risk or delay
- Build trust in project quality and operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and risk assessment checkpoints
- Incident or escalation communication during critical issues
- Compliance validation updates tied to product milestones

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work with Product Managers to assess security and compliance impact on roadmap decisions
- Partner with Technical Leads to review architecture decisions and control requirements
- Support Project Managers with escalation paths for high-risk issues and mitigation planning
- Coordinate with Developers on secure implementation practices and remediation tasks
- Provide release guidance with Release Managers for go/no-go decisions in sensitive environments

---

## Release Managers / Change Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment readiness, communication, and change control. They reduce operational risk around launches and ensure teams can hand off work with confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release timing, sequencing, and stakeholder communication
- Review deployment readiness, rollback plans, and contingency steps
- Track release dependencies, known issues, and rollout status
- Support post-deployment verification and incident follow-up
- Help keep release communication aligned across teams

### Goals
- Minimize release disruption and operational risk
- Improve clarity and predictability during deployment windows
- Ensure stakeholders are informed about impact and status

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch checklists
- Deployment updates and stakeholder announcements
- Post-release retrospectives and issue follow-up

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work with Project Managers to align release milestones, dependencies, and communication plans
- Partner with QA Leads to confirm quality gates and release confidence
- Support Product Managers with stakeholder messaging and launch timing
- Coordinate with Technical Leads on rollback readiness and deployment health
- Collaborate with Developers and support teams during go-live and recovery periods

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success or Stakeholder Liaison roles connect delivery work to customer and business impact. They translate user feedback, stakeholder needs, and operational realities into actionable project guidance.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback and communicate recurring issues or needs
- Help prioritize business and customer impact during planning and release decisions
- Support stakeholder communications and expectation management
- Identify risks related to adoption, service quality, and customer experience
- Provide insight into success metrics and user sentiment

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and adoption outcomes
- Keep stakeholder expectations aligned with project reality
- Ensure business context informs delivery decisions

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates and milestone check-ins
- Customer feedback summaries and product impact reviews
- Release and adoption communications with business teams

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work with Product Managers to translate customer needs into backlog priorities
- Support Project Managers with stakeholder communication plans and risk awareness
- Share customer impact insights with Developers and Technical Leads during planning and validation
- Coordinate with QA and Release teams on readiness and rollout communication
- Surface important business context that influences prioritization and trade-offs
